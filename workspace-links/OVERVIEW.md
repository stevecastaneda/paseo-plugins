Workspace Links puts the URLs your workspace runs on (dev servers, admin pages, emulators, docs) behind one Links button. List them in a `workspace-links.json` file at the workspace root, by hand or from your setup script.

Open the popover to see each link's name and URL, with an icon picked from its name. A green dot shows which links are running right now, and hovering a link lets you copy its URL. Click a row to open it.

The button is a composer pill by default. A setting moves it to the workspace header. Links open on the device you're viewing Paseo from, so `localhost` means that device. Needs Paseo 0.9 or later.
